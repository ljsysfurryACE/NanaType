Ink bamboo artwork used by NanaType's Ink Wash skin

Artist: Zheng Xie (Chinese, 1693-1765)
Work: Bamboo and Poem
Collection: The Metropolitan Museum of Art
Accession number: 1985.214.150
Credit: Gift of Ernest Erickson Foundation, 1985
Medium: Hanging scroll; ink on paper
Rights: Public Domain, The Met Open Access
Source: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/52946
Image: https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/api/collection/v1/iiif/52946/117149/main-image
Rights checked: 2026-10-15

bamboo.png is a detail of the lower-right bamboo shoot. The downloaded
image is 358 x 1200; the detail is the rectangle (145, 890, 339, 1175).
No lines, leaves or texture have been redrawn. RGB pixels are retained
exactly from the source crop, without recoloring or shape reconstruction.
Only the old paper background is made transparent to allow an overlay:
alpha = clamp((GaussianBlur(luminance, radius=20) - luminance - 7) * 3.8).
The separate collector seal in the bottom-right paper margin is excluded
from alpha in rectangle (160, 220, 194, 285); no bamboo pixels are redrawn.

The application fits this PNG without stretching or tiling in the right
margin, away from the dialogue text. It replaces the previous decorative
brush textures. This notice is retained when exporting or inheriting the
skin so the artwork's origin remains reviewable.
